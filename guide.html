How to Use Pariksa

Step-by-step instructions to navigate the high-precision disaster risk mapping system.

1

Open the Maps Page

Click Maps in the top navigation bar, or use the "Open WebGIS →" button on the homepage. The interactive map will load centred over Bandung City with a clean basemap from CartoDB.

2

Step 1 — Select a District

In the left sidebar, use the search box to find a kecamatan by name, or scroll the chip grid and click one to select it. To view all 30 districts at once, click the "All Bandung City" button at the top. Your selection highlights in red.

Andir Coblong Cicendo All Bandung City + 27 more…
3

Step 2 — Choose Hazard Type

Select the natural disaster category to analyse. Each hazard uses a dedicated spatial dataset computed at 1:5,000 scale.

Earthquake Flood Landslide Multi-Hazard
4

Step 3 — Apply Risk Class Filter (optional)

Filter the visible parcels by risk class to focus on specific areas. Selecting All Classes shows the complete dataset.

All Classes 🟢 Low 🟡 Medium 🔴 High
5

Render Map

Click the red "Render Map" button at the bottom of the sidebar. The map will fly to your selected area and load the WMS layer from GeoServer. A loading spinner appears while data is fetched. Once complete, the Active Layer card in the top-right confirms what is displayed.

6

Read & Explore the Results

Use the Risk Classification Legend in the sidebar to interpret the colour coding: Red = High, Yellow = Moderate, Green = Low. Click any parcel on the map to open the Feature Information popup showing all attribute data for that land parcel.

Disaster Indices Framework

>Our 1:5,000 scale spatial analysis is built upon a standardized and robust mathematical framework. We calculate urban risk by evaluating four core pillars.

01 — Hazard

Hazard Index (H)

We evaluate the physical threat levels across Bandung City. This includes calculating Peak Ground Acceleration (PGA) for earthquakes, Geomorphic Flood Indexes (GFI) for inundation, and slope susceptibility for landslides.

02 — Vulnerability

Vulnerability Index (V)

We assess the potential impact of disasters. This index measures social, physical, economic, and environmental exposure. For high-precision accuracy, we integrate parcel-level data such as building density, age, and function using MCDA-AHP weighting.

03 — Capacity

Capacity Index (C)

Risk is mitigated by readiness. This index measures the Regional Resilience Index (IKD) and Community Preparedness. It accounts for local knowledge, early warning systems, and disaster management policies.

04 — Risk

Risk Index (R)

The final spatial synthesis. By integrating the three indices using the mathematical formula R = H × (V / C), we determine the exact disaster risk classification (Low, Moderate, High) for every specific parcel of land.
