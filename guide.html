Disaster Framework

Core Variables

Risk

The mathematical probability of harmful consequences or expected losses in a specific area.

Hazard

A threatening natural event or phenomenon capable of causing damage and disruption.

Vulnerability

Physical, social, economic, or environmental weaknesses that increase susceptibility.

Capacity

Resources and strengths available to anticipate and manage disasters effectively.
